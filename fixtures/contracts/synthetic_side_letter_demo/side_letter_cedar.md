## Section 3 Management Fee Treatment

Notwithstanding Section 6.2 of the Partnership Agreement, solely with respect to Cedar Pension
Trust, any management fee included in a drawdown notice shall count toward and reduce,
pound-for-pound, the investment contribution and Called Capital otherwise called from Cedar
Pension Trust under that notice. The management fee shall therefore not increase the total cash
amount payable under the drawdown notice.
