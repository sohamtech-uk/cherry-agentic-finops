Bring your own evidence

PDFs + fund workbooks → automatically routed analysis.

Cherry first checks whether your files match the Ylookup sponsor datasets. Bank-statement working files and investor-level GLs are analysed in their native workflows; capital-call workbooks continue through the governed reconciliation flow. JSON cash evidence is optional.

  • PDF capital-call notices or bank statements
  • XLSX LP commitments, bank-statement working files, investor GLs or loader samples
  • Optional UTF-8 JSON cash/bank export for capital-call cash reconciliation
  • Protected deployments require the demo token supplied by the team

Cherry will auto-detect the workbook contract before applying workflow-specific controls.
